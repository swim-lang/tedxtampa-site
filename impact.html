Wall Text

Tampa cameready

TEDxTampa brought together leaders and original thinkers from healthcare, higher education, aviation, technology, public service, entertainment, entrepreneurship, and spiritual life. That range mattered. It allowed an idea about vulnerability to sit beside an idea about democracy, and a conversation about artificial intelligence to share space with one about mentorship, research, or faith.

The audience was part of that exchange. The sold-out response reflected a city interested not only in hearing accomplished people speak, but in considering what their ideas could mean for Tampa's organizations, neighborhoods, and next generation of leaders. The result was a local event with a wider horizon: grounded in Tampa, informed by global experience, designed to keep moving after the final applause.

Chapter 01 — Gather 18:00 EST

Tampa Museum of Art

The evening opened at the Tampa Museum of Art. Check-in and networking gave guests time to arrive, connect, and enter the event through one of the city's signature cultural spaces.

Chapter 02 — Listen 19:30 EST

Ferguson Hall at the Straz Center

The program continued at Ferguson Hall, where the TEDxTampa stage became the focus. Ten talks unfolded in a theater built for shared attention, giving each idea room to land before the next began.

Annex — Retrospective

SummitStartups

Summit Startups was created as an extension of TEDxTampa's interest in local ideas and shared opportunity — connecting promising startups with local and national investors while widening access for founders and communities often underrepresented in those rooms. The initiative focused on emerging work in artificial intelligence, blockchain, health technology, and other fields with the potential to shape what comes next.

●  A practical question: how can an event about ideas help more people move an idea forward?
