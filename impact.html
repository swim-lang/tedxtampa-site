A Sold-Out Return · May 2, 2025

Impact

After nearly a decade away, TEDxTampa returned with a stage built for the people and ideas shaping the region's future — and a city ready to listen.

Sold Out Tampa came ready

Every seat claimed for the return of TEDxTampa — a city interested in what ten ideas could mean for its organizations, neighborhoods, and next generation.

1,000 Seats made available

A city-scale gathering centered on ideas worth sharing, from check-in at the museum to the final applause.

2 Venues, one journey

The evening began at the Tampa Museum of Art and continued at Ferguson Hall at the Straz Center.

A Civic Event, Not Just a Stage

Tampa came ready

Leaders and original thinkers from healthcare, higher education, aviation, technology, public service, entertainment, and spiritual life — an idea about vulnerability beside an idea about democracy, AI in conversation with mentorship, research, and faith. The sold-out audience was part of that exchange.

Two Venues, One Journey

From the museum to the Straz

Check-in and networking opened the evening at the Tampa Museum of Art, one of the city's signature cultural spaces. The program continued at Ferguson Hall, where ten talks unfolded in a theater built for shared attention.
