Fig. 05 — The People Who Brought It Back 18:12 EST

No one reaches the top alone

Lift As You Climb begins with a belief that progress becomes more meaningful when it creates a path for someone else.

Wall Text — The Theme

Lift asyou climb

Mentorship, courage, access, conversation, and shared opportunity are not side effects of leadership; they are part of the work. The theme gave TEDxTampa's ten speakers a common starting point without asking them to arrive at the same conclusion — across healthcare, public service, education, technology, entertainment, business, and spiritual life.

For the audience, the invitation was equally direct: take what moves you, apply it where you are, and make the next step easier for someone coming behind you. Each talk offered a different view of how people lead, connect, and carry an idea forward.

A New ChapterFig. 04 — The Return

TEDxTampa returns after nearly a decade

The event was rebuilt around the energy of a changing city and a belief that Tampa deserved a shared stage for ideas with both local relevance and wider reach. The organizers brought experience from TEDx events and community-building work in India, Dubai, New York, and Abu Dhabi — but TEDxTampa remained unmistakably local in its audience, setting, and purpose.
