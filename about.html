About &
Team

An event theme. A leadership principle. A challenge to make progress more generous — built by a Tampa team with global TEDx experience.

The Theme

Lift As You Climb begins with a belief that progress becomes more meaningful when it creates a path for someone else. Mentorship, courage, access, conversation, and shared opportunity are not side effects of leadership — they are part of the work. The theme gave ten speakers a common starting point without asking them to arrive at the same conclusion.

In 2025, TEDxTampa returned after nearly a decade, rebuilt around the energy of a changing city. The evening began at the Tampa Museum of Art and moved to Ferguson Hall at the Straz Center — placing the event within Tampa's cultural core. The organizers brought experience from TEDx and community-building work in India, Dubai, New York, and Abu Dhabi, but TEDxTampa remained unmistakably local.

About TED

TED is a nonprofit organization devoted to discovering and spreading ideas. Through talks, media, and global programs, TED shares knowledge across disciplines and cultures. TEDx brings that spirit to independently organized local events.
